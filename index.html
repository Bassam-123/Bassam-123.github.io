About Me

Engineering high-reliability computational intelligence from exploratory research to edge deployment.

I am an AI/ML Engineer specializing in deep learning, LLM-powered systems, and Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) pipelines. I build end-to-end machine learning solutions — from mathematical modeling and experimentation to production deployment — delivering measurable impact in healthcare diagnostics and mission-critical public sector infrastructure.

Currently working at Centre for Development of Advanced Computing (C-DAC Chennai), I engineer national-scale AI systems including medical chest X-ray diagnostics, multi-GPU model serving pipelines, local enterprise RAG systems with rigorous guardrails, and automated LLM-as-a-Judge evaluation suites.

My work on XrayCAD is recognized among 196 global TB innovations by the Gates Foundation and the International Union Against Tuberculosis and Lung Disease, earning commendations from senior government healthcare leadership. I hold an M.Tech in Data Science from the Defence Institute of Advanced Technology (DIAT Pune) with research accepted at IEEE CVMI 2025 and CML 2026.
